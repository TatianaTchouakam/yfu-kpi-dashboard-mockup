Why two market shares? Gesamtmarktdaten covers every organisation in the German exchange market, so YFU's share there (8.67%) reflects its position across the whole market. The AJA dataset covers only nonprofit/associative organisations — a smaller, more comparable peer group — where YFU's share (33.76%) is much higher. Both are correct; they answer different questions, so they should never be mixed into a single "market share" number. MKT-007 (YFU Total Students Sent, historical volume) was rejected by the data owner as duplicating CTR-005/EXE-001 and is kept in the Catalogue for reference only — it does not appear on this dashboard.
